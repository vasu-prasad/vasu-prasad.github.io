About

I'm a fourth-year PhD student at the Institute of Astronomy, Cambridge working with Professor Cathie Clarke. My research focuses on protoplanetary discs and planet formation, using numerical simulations to study how planets form in young protoplanetary discs perturbed by encounters with other stars.
